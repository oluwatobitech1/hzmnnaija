Privacy policy

HymnNaija is designed to work with as little data collection as possible.

What is stored, and where

  • Favorites, recently viewed hymns, your theme choice and reading preferences (such as text size) are stored using your browser's localStorage, on your device only.
  • None of this information is sent to a server. HymnNaija has no backend and no database — there is nowhere for it to be sent.
  • Clearing your browser data, or using "Reset app data" in Settings, permanently removes this information.

Offline access

To let the app work offline, your device's browser caches the app's files and the hymn data you've opened. This cache lives on your device and is managed by your browser.

No accounts, no tracking, no ads

HymnNaija does not require sign-in, does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and does not share information with third parties.

Changes to this policy

If HymnNaija adds features that change how information is handled — for example, an optional way to sync favorites across devices — this page will be updated to describe it before that feature is turned on.
